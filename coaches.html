FAQ

Good questions

What does Levo cost coaches?

Nothing up front. Levo takes a percentage of subscription revenue — founding coaches keep the best share for as long as they're on the platform. Exact terms come with your acceptance.

Do I need certifications to coach?

Yes. Founding coaches carry a recognized personal training certification and their own liability insurance. We'll help you get set up if you're not there yet.

How do you choose coaches?

A human reads every application and looks at your content, your audience and how you coach. Small cohort, picked slowly, on purpose.

Is anything live today?

Honestly: signups. Applications are real and reviewed; the platform — payments, plans, messaging — arrives at launch, and founding coaches shape what ships first.
