FAQ

Good questions

What does Repwave cost coaches?

Nothing up front. Repwave takes a percentage of subscription revenue — and founding coaches lock in the best rev share for life. Exact terms come with your acceptance.

When do fans get access?

We're launching coach by coach. Join a coach's waitlist (Vice Wave's is open now) and you'll get early access before their public launch.

How does coach vetting work?

Every application is reviewed by a human. We look at your content, your audience and your coaching chops before the verified badge goes on your profile.

Do I need certifications to coach?

Certifications help but aren't the whole story — we care that you can actually coach and that your content backs it up. Tell us everything in your application.

What will fans pay?

Each coach sets their own subscription price. Early waitlist members lock in founding-fan pricing with their coach.

Is anything live today?

Honestly: signups. Coach applications and fan waitlists are real and reviewed; the full platform — payments, plans, tracking, DMs — arrives at launch. Early lists get in first.
